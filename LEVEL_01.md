# LEVEL 01 — SUBNET ONE

## Goal
Complete the first subnet and return with its reward / artefact.

## Target Runtime
10–15 minutes

## Structure

### 1. Hub intro
- Player starts in the central Bittensor hub
- 128 nodes/gates are visible
- only one is active
- TWIF enters the first subnet

### 2. Arrival / explore
- Short intro area
- establish the art style and world
- player sees that this is a neo-samurai Bittensor district

### 3. Max Sensei tutorial
- Meet Max
- learn movement
- learn sword attack
- learn dodge / interact
- one or two easy enemies

### 4. Objective begins
- TWIF is told to reach the subnet objective / control point / artefact zone
- player explores streets / small district

### 5. Subnet Summer van
- player discovers and enters the Subnet Summer van
- short driving section through the subnet world
- some environmental chaos / obstacles / fun motion

### 6. Chase / action set piece
- brief pursuit or race to the final area
- should be arcade-like and readable

### 7. Final encounter
- leave van
- short combat encounter
- destroy or complete the subnet objective
- claim the subnet reward / artefact

### 8. Return / payoff
- return to hub
- the completed node lights up
- UI or scene shows: 1 / 128 complete

## Gameplay Pillars
- clear fun movement
- satisfying simple combat
- one memorable driving moment
- lots of environmental references
- no overcomplicated systems

## Environmental Lore Ideas
- Mog posters
- Const shrine / statue
- Sam Dare relic billboard
- TAO shop / convenience store
- fake newspapers
- little subnet references
- hidden jokes and signs
